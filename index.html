Digital banking · Renmoney

Delivery transformation

Context. Digital banking environment with competing priorities, fragmented workflows, and low delivery predictability.

My role. Owned the product delivery system and operating model across product, engineering, analytics, and QA.

Problem. Backlog growth, unclear prioritization, weak alignment between teams, delivery inefficiency.

What I changed.

  • Redesigned backlog governance and prioritization model
  • Introduced structured intake and request management
  • Transitioned backend team to Kanban workflow
  • Rebuilt Jira workflows and delivery structure
  • Introduced RFC process for technical alignment
  • Defined collaboration model across teams
  • Standardized delivery processes across engineering and analytics

Outcome. +34% delivery speed, −63% active backlog, improved execution predictability and reduced cross-team ambiguity.

Outcome. +30% candidate inflow, −92% operational cost, +2 Candidate NPS, improved efficiency across distributed teams.

How I lead

Leadership approach

I don’t lead with slides—I lead with decisions: what ships first, what we stop, and how we know we succeeded. Strategy only matters when teams share one prioritization model, one delivery cadence, and metrics that change when we change the product—not when reporting changes.

Clarity on bets → explicit trade-offs → execution design → delivery → measurable outcomes
